Common Questions

How do I use a text tool?
Choose a tool, enter or paste your text, then use the result or copy it. Counter and Case Converter are available without a subscription.

What does Textura Pro include?
Textura Pro unlocks Clean & Sort, Find & Replace, Encoders, and Generators. Weekly and monthly plans unlock the same features for their subscription period.

How do I restore a subscription?
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Is my data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy. Text entered into the tools is processed on your device and is not sent to us.

How do I delete my data?
Tool input is not stored on our servers. Removing the app deletes its local preferences.
