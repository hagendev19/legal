Privacy Policy

Textura · Last updated August 20, 2026

Data Collection

Textura does not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information. No account registration is required.

Text Processing

Text you enter or paste into the counter, case converter, cleaning, replacement, encoding, and generation tools is processed on your device. It is not sent to us.

Local Storage

App preferences, such as the haptics setting, are stored locally on your device. Removing the app removes this locally stored data.

Network and Purchases

The app may connect to infrastructure required to load its interface and to Apple services to display subscription products, process purchases, and restore purchases. These connections do not include the text you process in the app.

Third Parties

No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs are included. Subscription transactions are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

Textura is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
