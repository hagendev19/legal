Privacy Policy

Lumio · Last updated August 20, 2026

Data Collection

Lumio does not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information. No account registration is required.

Light Tools

Colour selections, brightness choices, gradient scenes, signal modes, and party-mode settings are processed on your device. Lumio does not transmit these choices to us.

Local Storage

App preferences and saved favourite colours are stored locally on your device. Removing the app removes this locally stored data.

Network and Purchases

The app may connect to infrastructure required to load its interface and to Apple services to display subscription products, process purchases, and restore purchases. These connections do not include your light and colour selections.

Third Parties

No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs are included. Subscription transactions are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

Lumio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
