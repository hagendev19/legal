Common Questions

How do I use Lumio?
Choose Flashlight or Colour Light from the home screen, then adjust the on-screen light. These two tools are available without a subscription.

What does Lumio Pro include?
Lumio Pro unlocks the full colour panel, saved colours, gradient scenes, on-screen signal modes, and party mode. Weekly and monthly plans unlock the same features.

Signal mode is not flashing the camera light.
This is expected. Lumio’s signal and party modes use the screen as the light source.

How do I restore a subscription?
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Is my data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy. Your light and colour selections are not sent to us.

How do I delete my data?
Removing the app deletes its locally stored preferences and saved colours.
