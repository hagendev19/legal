Privacy Policy

Finora · Last updated August 20, 2026

Data Collection

Finora does not collect, store, or share personally identifiable information. No account registration is required.

Financial Calculations

Loan amounts, interest rates, terms, contributions, and other values you enter are processed on your device. They are not transmitted to us. Results are estimates for planning and educational use and are not financial advice.

Local Storage

App preferences and calculations you save to History are stored locally on your device. You can clear History in the app; removing the app removes all local data.

Network and Purchases

The app may connect to infrastructure required to load its interface and to Apple services to display subscription products, process purchases, and restore purchases. These connections do not include the financial values you enter.

Third Parties

No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs are included. Subscription transactions are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

Finora is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
