Common Questions

How do I calculate a loan payment?
Open Loan / EMI, enter the principal, annual interest rate, and term, then review the estimated monthly payment and total interest.

What does Finora Pro include?
Finora Pro unlocks compound growth and savings projections, amortization schedules, loan comparison, ROI and inflation tools, tip splitting, and saved History.

Are the results financial advice?
No. Finora provides mathematical estimates for planning and educational use. Verify important decisions with your lender or a qualified financial professional.

How do I restore a subscription?
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Is my data private?
Yes — see our Privacy Policy. Values entered into calculators are processed on your device and are not sent to us.

How do I delete my data?
Clear saved calculations from History. Removing the app deletes all locally stored data.
